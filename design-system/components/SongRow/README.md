# SongRow

One song in a list.

- 58 tall, cover 44 with `radius-art`, title in `body`, artist in `small` `muted`, time on the right in `small` with tabular numbers.
- The playing song has a `soft` row, `blue-text` title in semibold and says Playing.
- Songs without art use the `placeholder` cover with a `primary` note.
- Tap plays from this list. Hold opens the song menu.
