# PlayerControls

The bottom half of Now playing, on `hero`.

- Progress is a 6 track, white at 16 percent, with the played part in `ring` and a 16 white thumb. Drag or tap anywhere.
- Times in `small` `hero-text`, elapsed left, remaining right with a minus.
- Shuffle and repeat 44 at the edges, `hero-text` when off, `ring` on a soft pill when on. Repeat one shows a small 1.
- Previous and next 56 with 32 filled icons. Play 76 in `primary`.
