# MiniPlayer

The song that is loaded, floating above the tab bar.

- 64 tall, 12 from the screen sides, glass with `shadow-float` and `radius-sheet`.
- Cover 44, title in `title`, artist in `small` `muted`. Play or pause and next on the right, filled icons in `ink`.
- A 2 line progress bar along the bottom in `primary`.
- Tapping the song opens Now playing.
